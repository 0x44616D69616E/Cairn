                  Before you head out

                  Cairn only works offline for areas you've downloaded ahead of time. Satellite, topo, and trail imagery for a region needs to be downloaded first - from the download icon in the menu - while you still have signal.

                  Set up storage?

                  Cairn can back up your flags, routes, tracks, and settings to a folder on your device, so you can restore them later or move them to a new phone. You can do this later from Settings instead.

                  Choose a folder

                  Cairn will create a "Cairn" folder inside whichever one you pick.
